Scroll Sense · Android app

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 September 2026. Applies to the Scroll Sense Android app (package com.dupati.scrollsense), published on Google Play.

01Overview

Scroll Sense is a screen-time awareness app. It shows a small floating bubble over other apps so you can see, at a glance, how long you've spent in the apps you've chosen to watch. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

02Information we collect

App usage data — always, stored on your device only

To show your time on each app, Scroll Sense reads which apps you open and for how long, using Android's Usage Access permission. This data is processed and stored entirely on your device in app-private storage. It is never sent anywhere unless you turn on Cloud Sync (below).

Google account info — only if you sign in for Cloud Sync

Cloud Sync is optional and off by default. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account ID through Firebase Authentication, used only to identify your synced data.

Synced settings & usage history — only if signed in

When Cloud Sync is on, your app settings (watched apps, reminder timing, bubble colour, mascot, messages, limits) and your usage history (daily totals, per-app totals, session log) are stored in Google Firestore under your account, so they can be restored on another device or after reinstalling.

News headlines — only if the news feature is on

If you set a news interval (off by default), Scroll Sense periodically requests one or more public BBC News RSS feeds — whichever topics you've chosen in the News screen — to show a headline on the bubble or in that screen's headline list. These are plain, anonymous requests for a public feed — no account info, device identifier or usage data is sent to BBC, and nothing about which headlines or topics you view is sent back to us or stored anywhere. Tapping a headline opens the article in your browser.

Stock symbols — only if you add any in the Stocks screen

When you search for a company or symbol to add, whatever you type is sent as a plain search query to Yahoo Finance's public search endpoint so matching symbols can be shown to you. Once you track a stock, Scroll Sense requests that symbol's price from Yahoo Finance's public quote data, and stock-related headlines from Google News' public search feed, so it can show you the price, day change and recent news for stocks you've chosen to follow. No account info, device identifier or usage data is sent in any of these requests, and the list of stocks you track is stored only on your device (or in Firestore under your account if Cloud Sync is on), never on our servers. Tapping a stock headline opens the article in your browser.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera input, the content of other apps, SMS, or advertising identifiers. Scroll Sense shows no ads and includes no third-party ad or analytics SDKs.

03How we use it

  • To calculate and display your time on watched apps, and to show reminders and limit alerts you've configured.
  • To back up and restore your settings and usage history across devices, only when Cloud Sync is turned on.
  • To show a periodic news headline on the bubble, only when the news feature is turned on.
  • To show prices and headlines for stock symbols you've chosen to track, only when you've added any in the Stocks screen.
  • To process the one-time "Pro" upgrade purchase through Google Play Billing. Google handles all payment details — we never receive or store your card information.

06Data retention & deletion

Uninstalling Scroll Sense deletes all data stored on your device immediately. Signing out of Cloud Sync stops further syncing but does not by itself delete data already stored in the cloud. To request deletion of your cloud-synced settings and history, contact us at the email below and we'll remove it.

07Children's privacy

Scroll Sense is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app's Parental Lock feature is designed for a device owner to restrict changes to their own settings, not to collect data about a child.

08Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and, for material changes, note it within the app.
