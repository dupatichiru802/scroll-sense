Delete your account and data

For the Scroll Sense Android app (com.dupati.scrollsense), developed by Dupati.

How to delete your account

  1. 1 Open Scroll Sense and scroll to the "Sync settings" card, then tap "Sign out" if you're signed in with Google.
  2. 2 Email us a deletion request at scrollsensedata@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Scroll Sense data".
  3. 3 We delete your cloud data — your synced settings and usage history stored in Firestore — within 30 days, and reply to confirm.

Uninstalling the app immediately deletes everything stored on your device; it does not, by itself, delete anything already synced to the cloud, which is why the email step above is needed for that copy.

What gets deleted, and what's kept

DataOn request
Your account link (Google sign-in identifier)Deleted
Synced settings (watched apps, reminders, bubble colour, mascot, messages, limits)Deleted
Synced usage history (daily totals, per-app totals, session log)Deleted
On-device usage data (never leaves your phone unless synced)Deleted immediately on uninstall, or on request

We don't retain a backup copy beyond what's needed to process the request, and we keep nothing longer than 30 days after a confirmed deletion request.
